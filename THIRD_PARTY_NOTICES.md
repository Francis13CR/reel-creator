# Third-party notices

Reel Creator is an original, brand-agnostic production system. Its skill
structure and several motion principles were inspired by these MIT-licensed
projects:

- `charlie947/motion-graphics-skills` — <https://github.com/charlie947/motion-graphics-skills>
- `haidrrrry/claude-remotion-skill` — <https://github.com/haidrrrry/claude-remotion-skill>

The adaptations change the workflow to portable short-form video production,
word-level synchronization, zero-empty-center layouts, SFX design and QA gates.
